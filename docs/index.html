Scene Visualization

AI-Powered Image Generation

Automatically generates scene art from your story text. Supports NovelAI V3, V4, and V4.5 models, plus Venice, Perchance, and Puter.js providers. Per-character structured prompts prevent attribute leakage between characters in multi-character scenes.

Lore Management

Intelligent Lorebook Scanning

AI scans your story and auto-generates lorebook entries for characters, locations, items, factions, and concepts. Detects duplicates, tracks relationships, and keeps entries up to date as your story evolves. Supports hybrid LLM scanning with parallel providers.

Screenshot coming soon
RPG Tracking

LitRPG Character Sheets

Auto-detects RPG elements in your story — stats, classes, levels, inventory, quests. Full character sheets with auto-generated portraits, party management, and NPC tracking. Supports D&D, cultivation, GameLit, MMORPG, and survival systems.

Screenshot coming soon
Narration

Multi-Voice Text-to-Speech

Assigns unique voices to each character. Distinguishes narration from dialogue, detects speaker attribution from context, and plays back with per-character voice acting. 28+ voices across NovelAI and Venice TTS providers with custom voice seed blending.

And More

Every tool you need to enhance your NovelAI experience.

📷

Media Gallery

Auto-saves generated images and videos with thumbnails. Lightbox viewer with prompt re-use, download, and per-story organization.

📚

Scene Timeline

Detects scene boundaries and chapter structure from your story text. Interactive scene explorer with character tracking per scene.

🎨

Character Portraits

Auto-generates character portraits from lorebook data. Portrait albums track visual history. Deterministic prompts from visual profiles.

🧠

Memory Manager

Context-aware memory editing with token counting. Read and write NovelAI's memory field directly from the companion panel.

⚙

Per-Story Settings

Independent configuration for each story — TTS voices, image provider, model, resolution, art style, and scene prompt settings.

🎬

Video Generation

Generate scene videos from your story with Venice AI. Async queue with progress tracking, automatic polling, and gallery integration.
